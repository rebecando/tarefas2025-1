Model

Two-axle robot in the vertical plane, rigid wheels, quasi-static regime (accelerations neglected). At each obstacle the critical instant is analyzed: the front wheel touches the edge while still resting on the lower surface. Geometry (chassis scraping, bumper, resting on stair nosings) is checked along the whole path.

Rules used

Each section number opens the RoboCupJunior Rescue Maze 2027 rules (draft) at its page.

  • 4.1.1 All field measurements have a ±10 % tolerance.
  • 4.1.2 Tiles are 28 × 28 cm; walls at least 12.5 cm high (4.1.3).
  • 4.1.5 Up to 3 mm height deviation and 3 mm gaps between tiles.
  • 4.2 Hazard Zone: enclosed, red tile at each entrance; the Hazard Variant rules apply inside.
  • 4.4.1 Speed bumps fixed to the floor, up to 1 cm, never on ramps or stairs. Hazard Variant: up to 2 cm and allowed on ramps.
  • 4.4.3 Debris only in the Hazard Zone, loose, up to 1 cm (not simulated here).
  • 4.4.4 Ramps up to 25°, no peak-like structure, always ending on a horizontal tile. Hazard Variant: up to 30°.
  • 4.4.5 Stairs as wide as the tiles, steps up to 2 cm, incline up to 25° between steps, ending on a horizontal tile. Peak-like stairs: top step 15 cm to less than one tile. Hazard Variant: up to 30°.
  • 6.7 Scored ramp or stair: 10 pts, when the robot reaches a horizontal tile other than the one it started from. Speed bump tile: 5 pts.
  • 6.8.2 Exit bonus adds 5 pts per scored ramp and per scored stair.
